Channels come from [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/database) (its new channels are added every night for the
followed countries) and from people who add them here. A channel does **not** need to be in iptv-org.

::card-group
  :::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-circle-plus
  title: channels/add
  to: https://github.com/worldwide-tv-logos/database/issues/new?template=07_channels_add.yml
  target: _blank
  ---
  A channel that is not on the website yet. Also linked on every country page (**Add it**).
  :::

  :::card
  ---
  icon: i-lucide-pencil
  title: channels/edit
  to: https://github.com/worldwide-tv-logos/database/issues/new?template=08_channels_edit.yml
  target: _blank
  ---
  New name, website, other names, main channel, closed, or one of its IDs.
  :::
::

::warning
**Look for the channel first** with the [search](/countries) (:kbd{value="meta"} :kbd{value="K"}), by its name and
its former names. A renamed channel is corrected with channels/edit, never added again: it keeps its ID and its logo.
::

## channels/add

::field-group
  :::field{name="Channel name" type="text" required}
  The name shown on air or on the channel's website.
  :::

  :::field{name="Country" type="code" required}
  Two-letter code of the country it is broadcast in: `fr`, `de`, `uk` for the United Kingdom.
  :::

  :::field{name="Website" type="URL" required}
  The channel's own page, showing its name: it is used to check the channel and to find its logo.
  :::

  :::field{name="Other names" type="text"}
  Separated by `;`.
  :::

  :::field{name="Main channel" type="ch_ ID"}
  For an edition only (a regional or local version): our ID of the channel it is a version of.
  :::

  :::field{name="iptv-org ID" type="text"}
  When the channel is in iptv-org: `France3.fr`, or `France3.fr@Alsace` for an edition.
  :::

  :::field{name="Wikidata ID" type="text"}
  `Q` and digits, when the channel has a Wikidata entry.
  :::
::

::callout{icon="i-lucide-key-round" color="info"}
**You never choose the ID.** The channel gets its `ch_` ID automatically the night after approval, and the issue is
closed with it. Then add its logo with [logos/add](/docs/contribute), or let the weekly search find it.
::

A request is refused when a channel of the same country already has this name or one of these other names (use
channels/edit), when the main channel or an ID is not valid, or when the iptv-org or Wikidata ID already belongs to
another channel.

## channels/edit

Give the **Channel ID** and only the fields that change, with a **Reason** and a link that shows it (the channel's
website, a news article).

::field-group
  :::field{name="Channel name" type="text"}
  Its new name: the slug follows, the ID and the logo address don't change.
  :::

  :::field{name="Other names" type="text"}
  All its other names, separated by `;`: they replace the current ones.
  :::

  :::field{name="Website" type="URL"}
  Its new website.
  :::

  :::field{name="Main channel" type="ch_ ID"}
  For an edition, the channel it is a version of.
  :::

  :::field{name="Status" type="active | closed"}
  `closed` when the channel stopped broadcasting.
  :::

  :::field{name="iptv-org ID, Wikidata ID" type="text"}
  Its ID in these projects, when it was missing.
  :::
::

::note
Once a channel has been edited here, the nightly follow-up of iptv-org no longer overwrites its name, other names and
website: your correction stays.
::

## Editions

An edition is a regional or local version of a channel, with its own programmes at times: France 3 Alsace is an
edition of France 3. Add it with channels/add and its **Main channel**. It gets its own logo only when its logo
differs from the channel's (its region written under the brand); otherwise the channel's logo is used.

Quality versions (`HD`, `4K`) and timeshift versions (`+1`) are not editions: don't add them.
