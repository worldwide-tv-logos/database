## Our channel ID

```txt
ch_1r840gxdj5
```

`ch_` followed by 10 random characters (digits and lowercase letters, without `i`, `l`, `o` and `u`, which are easy
to confuse). It is made here when the channel is added, by a script, never chosen by a person.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-shield-check" color="success"}
**It never changes and is never reused.** A channel renamed, moved to another country, or taken from another source
keeps its ID, and so the address of its logo. Store this ID, not the name.
::

It carries no meaning on purpose: a name, a country or a project's ID can change, a random ID has no reason to.

## The slug

A readable name, unique, made from the channel's name and country: `france-3.fr`, `bfmtv-lyon.fr`, `tf1-2.fr` for a
second channel of the same name. It is shown on the website and accepted by the search and the API, **but it changes
when the channel is renamed**: never use it as a key.

## The fields of a channel

::field-group
  :::field{name="id" type="string" required}
  Our channel ID, e.g. `ch_1r840gxdj5`.
  :::

  :::field{name="slug" type="string" required}
  Readable name, e.g. `france-3.fr` (may change on a rename).
  :::

  :::field{name="name" type="string" required}
  The name shown on air or on the channel's website.
  :::

  :::field{name="aliases" type="string[]"}
  Its other names (former names, short names, names in other languages).
  :::

  :::field{name="country" type="string" required}
  Two-letter code of the country it is broadcast in, lowercase (`uk` for the United Kingdom).
  :::

  :::field{name="status" type="active | closed | merged" required}
  `active`; `closed` when the channel stopped; `merged` when it became another channel of this database (then
  `parent` is that channel).
  :::

  :::field{name="parent" type="string"}
  For an edition, the ID of its main channel. For a merged channel, the ID of the channel it became.
  :::

  :::field{name="website" type="string"}
  The channel's own website, used to check the channel and to find its current logo.
  :::
::

## Editions

An edition is a regional or local version of a channel: France 3 Alsace is an edition of France 3. It has its own ID
and its `parent` is the main channel.

::steps{level="3"}
### Its own logo when it differs

An edition whose logo shows its region (`France 3 Alsace` written under the brand) gets its own logo.

### Otherwise the channel's logo

An edition without a logo of its own uses its channel's. [`channels.json`](/docs/getting-started/api#channelsjson)
writes it out for you (`logo_from` names the channel it comes from).
::

## Renamed, closed, merged

| What happens | Here |
|---|---|
| The channel is renamed | Same ID, same logo address; `name` and `slug` follow. |
| Its iptv-org ID changes | Same ID; the new iptv-org ID is mapped to it, the old one is kept as a former ID. |
| It stops | `status: closed`; its logo stays available. |
| It becomes another channel already listed | `status: merged`, `parent` = the channel it became. |
| It gets a new branding | Same address, new image; `updated` in [`logos.json`](/docs/getting-started/api#logosjson) changes. |

## Other projects' IDs

Two other IDs are kept with each channel, when it has them:

::card-group
  :::card
  ---
  icon: i-simple-icons-github
  title: iptv-org
  to: https://github.com/iptv-org/database
  target: _blank
  ---
  `France3.fr`, or `France3.fr@Alsace` for an edition (a "feed" there). The shared list of TV channels used by many
  playlists and guides. Its new, renamed and closed channels are followed every night; a renamed one keeps our ID.
  :::

  :::card
  ---
  icon: i-simple-icons-wikidata
  title: Wikidata
  to: https://www.wikidata.org
  target: _blank
  ---
  `Q…`: the entry of the channel in Wikidata, a stable ID, also used to find the official logo and its dates.
  :::
::

::note
**TV guide IDs are not stored**, apart from the aliases confirmed by review: guides change their IDs, and following
them by hand would never end. They are matched again every night instead: see
[TV guides](/docs/getting-started/tv-guides).
::
