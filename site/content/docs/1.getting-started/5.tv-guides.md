A TV guide (EPG, XMLTV) gives each channel an ID of its own, such as `TF1+1.fr` or `France3-Alsace.fr`. You don't
need to build your own matching with our channels: it is done here every night, and published in
[`guides.json`](/docs/getting-started/api#guidesjson).

## Try it

::guide-lookup
::

The search above, and the one of the whole website (:kbd{value="meta"} :kbd{value="K"}), find a channel by its
guide IDs from a score of 0.9. An edition's guide IDs lead to its channel's page, where the edition is listed.

## How guide channels are matched

Every night, the channel list of each guide is read again and matched with our channels, in this order:

::steps{level="3"}
### A confirmed alias

The guide ID was linked to a channel by review ([aliases](/docs/contribute/aliases)). Score **1.0**; it always wins.

### The same iptv-org ID

Many guides use the [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/database) ID of the channel (`TF1.fr`), case aside.
Score **0.95**.

### The same name in the country

One channel only of the guide's country has this name. Quality and timeshift marks are left aside (`TF1 HD`,
`TF1 + 1` and `TF1` are the same channel, with the same logo), and so is a final `TV`. A guide without channel names
gives its ID's (`France3.fr`). Score **0.9**.

### Another name, or another country

The name is only one of the channel's other names, or the channel is from another country. Score **0.7**: likely,
not sure (other names are sometimes loose).

### Ambiguous

Two or three channels have the name: each is listed with the score **0.5**. A name shared by more than three channels
is left out.
::

::tip
Use the matches of **0.9 and more** directly. Show the others to a person to choose, or ask for an
[alias](/docs/contribute/aliases) once you know the right channel: it becomes 1.0 for everyone.
::

## Guides read

| Guide | Name in `guides.json` | Channel IDs |
|---|---|---|
| [iptv-epg](https://iptv-epg.org) | `iptv-epg-<cc>`, one per followed country | e.g. `TF1+1.fr`, `France3-Alsace.fr` |
| [xmltv.se](https://xmltv.se) | `xmltv-se` | e.g. `3sat.de`, `20.mediaset.it` |
| other guides | the `source` of their [aliases](/docs/getting-started/api#aliasesjson) | only the confirmed aliases |

::callout{icon="i-lucide-refresh-cw" color="info"}
**A guide that renames its IDs is followed without anything to do**: its new list is matched the next night. That is
why guide IDs are not stored with the channels (except confirmed aliases).
::

## Your guide is not listed

Match it by name: give the channel name and its country to the [search](/countries), or look the name up in
[`channels.json`](/docs/getting-started/api#channelsjson) (`name` and `aliases`, in the same country). The rules
above apply the same way.
