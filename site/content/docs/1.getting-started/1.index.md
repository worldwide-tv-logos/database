Worldwide TV Logos is an open database of TV channels and their logos. Every channel has its own ID here (`ch_`
and 10 characters) that never changes, and every logo is published under an address derived from it. Each channel
also gives its [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/database) ID when it has one, the shared list of TV channels
used by TV guides and playlists.

## What you get

- **Transparent background**, trimmed to the drawing, high resolution (SVG originals rendered at 1200 px, longest
  side 500 to 2000 px, never upscaled).
- **Readable everywhere**: a white variant for logos that are black, grey or very dark (navy letters), so they stay
  readable on dark screens; a logo made for dark screens (white letters) gets a dark version for light ones.
- **One stable address per logo**, derived from our channel ID: a channel renamed keeps it.
- **Current logos only**: old brandings are refused (dated by Wikidata or by the file name), and every logo is
  checked by eye on contact sheets before it is published.
- **Aliases** for the IDs used by TV guides (EPG).

## Channels and editions

A **channel** is, e.g., France 3 (`ch_1r840gxdj5`, iptv-org `France3.fr`). An **edition** is a regional or local
version of a channel, with its own ID, e.g. France 3 Alsace (`ch_dhk02zjhwa`, iptv-org `France3.fr@Alsace`). An
edition gets its own logo only when it differs from the channel's; otherwise it uses the channel logo. Quality
versions (HD, 4K) never get a logo of their own.

::note
New channels of iptv-org are added here every night; channels are not limited to iptv-org. See
[Contribute](/docs/contribute).
::
