Worldwide TV Logos is an open database of TV channel logos. Every logo is attached to a channel of
[iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/database), the shared list of TV channels used by TV guides and playlists,
and is published under an address derived from the channel ID.

## What you get

- **Transparent background**, trimmed to the drawing, high resolution (SVG originals rendered at 1200 px, longest
  side 500 to 2000 px, never upscaled).
- **Readable everywhere**: a white variant for logos that are black, grey or very dark (navy letters), so they stay
  readable on dark screens; a logo made for dark screens (white letters) gets a dark version for light ones.
- **One stable address per logo**, derived from the iptv-org ID: no guessing, no renaming.
- **Current logos only**: old brandings are refused (dated by Wikidata or by the file name), and every logo is
  checked by eye on contact sheets before it is published.
- **Aliases** for the IDs used by TV guides (EPG) that differ from iptv-org.

## Channels and editions

A **channel** is an iptv-org channel, e.g. `France3.fr`. An **edition** is one of its feeds: a regional or local
version, e.g. `France3.fr@Alsace`. An edition gets its own logo only when it differs from the channel's; otherwise
it uses the channel logo. Quality versions (HD, 4K) never get a logo of their own.

::note
Channel names, countries and categories come from iptv-org. A channel that is not in iptv-org must be added there
first: see [Contribute](/docs/contribute).
::
