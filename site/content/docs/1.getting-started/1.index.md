Worldwide TV Logos is an open database of TV channels and their logos. Every channel has **its own ID here**
(`ch_` and 10 characters) that never changes, and every logo is published at an address made from that ID. The
channels also carry the IDs other projects use ([iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/database),
[Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org)), and the channels of public TV guides are matched with them every night: you can
find a logo from what you already have.

## What you get

::field-group
  :::field{name="Transparent and trimmed" type="PNG"}
  Every logo is a PNG on a transparent background, trimmed to the drawing (no margin), its longest side between
  500 and 2,000 px. SVG originals are rendered at 1,200 px; images are never upscaled.
  :::

  :::field{name="Readable on any screen" type="variant"}
  A logo that is black, grey or very dark gets a **white version** for dark backgrounds (`<id>.white.png`). A logo
  made of white letters gets a dark version, so it stays readable on light backgrounds.
  :::

  :::field{name="One stable address" type="URL"}
  `logos/<id>.png`, from our channel ID. A channel renamed or moved to another country keeps its ID; a new image of
  the logo keeps the address.
  :::

  :::field{name="Current logos only" type="review"}
  Old brandings are refused (dated by Wikidata or by the file name), and every logo is looked at on a dark and a light
  background before it is published.
  :::

  :::field{name="Found from what you hold" type="lookup"}
  Our ID, a slug, an iptv-org or Wikidata ID, a TV guide channel ID, or a name: the
  [search](/countries) and the [API](/docs/getting-started/api) accept them all.
  :::
::

## Key words

::tabs
  :::tabs-item{label="Channel" icon="i-lucide-tv"}
  A TV channel, e.g. **France 3** (`ch_1r840gxdj5`, iptv-org `France3.fr`). It has one ID here, a readable
  slug (`france-3.fr`), a country, a status, and usually a logo.
  :::

  :::tabs-item{label="Edition" icon="i-lucide-map-pin"}
  A regional or local version of a channel, with its own ID, e.g. **France 3 Alsace** (`ch_dhk02zjhwa`, iptv-org
  `France3.fr@Alsace`), whose main channel is France 3. It has a logo of its own only when it differs from the
  channel's; otherwise the channel's logo is used.
  :::

  :::tabs-item{label="Variant" icon="i-lucide-contrast"}
  The **white** version of a logo, for dark backgrounds: `<id>.white.png`. It exists only when the logo would be hard
  to read on a dark screen.
  :::

  :::tabs-item{label="Alias" icon="i-lucide-link"}
  The ID a TV guide (EPG) uses for a channel, confirmed by review, e.g. `France3-Alsace.fr` in the guide
  `iptv-epg-fr`. Other guide IDs are matched automatically every night (see
  [TV guides](/docs/getting-started/tv-guides)).
  :::
::

::note
Quality versions (HD, 4K) and timeshift versions (+1) are the same channel: they never get an ID or a logo of their
own. Channels are not limited to iptv-org: anyone can [add a channel](/docs/contribute/channels).
::
