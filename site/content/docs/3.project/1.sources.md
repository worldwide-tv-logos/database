## Where the logos come from

::card-group
  :::card{icon="i-lucide-users" title="Contributors"}
  Logos sent with the [forms](/docs/contribute), checked and reviewed one by one.
  :::

  :::card{icon="i-simple-icons-github" title="iptv-org" to="https://github.com/iptv-org/database" target="_blank"}
  The logos listed with each channel of the shared list of TV channels.
  :::

  :::card{icon="i-lucide-calendar-range" title="iptv-epg" to="https://iptv-epg.org" target="_blank"}
  The logos of its TV guides, by country.
  :::

  :::card{icon="i-simple-icons-wikidata" title="Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons" to="https://www.wikidata.org" target="_blank"}
  Official logos with their dates (old ones are refused), and Commons files named after the channel.
  :::

  :::card{icon="i-simple-icons-wikipedia" title="Wikipedia"}
  The logo of the channel's article, in the country's languages, then in English.
  :::

  :::card{icon="i-lucide-images" title="picons and xmltv.se"}
  Collections of channel logos ([picons](https://github.com/picons/picons),
  [xmltv.se](https://gitlab.com/xmltv-se/open-source/channel-logos)), matched by name.
  :::

  :::card{icon="i-lucide-globe" title="The channel's website"}
  Its current logo, read from the page (and from a headless browser when the page is drawn by scripts).
  :::

  :::card{icon="i-lucide-search" title="Image search" to="https://duckduckgo.com" target="_blank"}
  A DuckDuckGo image search for each channel still without a logo (large images only, no social networks or stock
  images). Asked first, and the least sure: every result is checked by eye before it is kept.
  :::
::

## Preparation

Every candidate, whatever its source, goes through the same steps:

::steps{level="3"}
### Rendered

SVG originals are rendered at 1,200 px; bitmaps are never upscaled.

### Trimmed and cleaned

The image is trimmed to the drawing. Its white background is removed, unless the logo would be unreadable on a dark
screen without it (a brand block, white letters). The PNG is compressed without loss.

### Measured

The longest side must be at least 300 px once trimmed (2,000 px at most). A logo under 1,000 px from a bitmap is looked for again every week in a sharper version.

### Given a version for the other background

A dark logo on a transparent background gets a **white version** for dark screens; a logo made of white letters gets
a dark version for light screens.
::

## Choice and review

::field-group
  :::field{name="The best candidate" type="choice"}
  SVG first, then the largest.
  :::

  :::field{name="No old brandings" type="check"}
  A Wikidata logo with an end date, or a file named after a past period, is refused.
  :::

  :::field{name="No shared drawing" type="check"}
  The same drawing found for several channels (perceptual fingerprint) is a doubt, not a logo: a brand logo is not the
  logo of each of its numbered channels.
  :::

  :::field{name="Looked at" type="review"}
  Every logo is looked at on contact sheets, on a dark and a light background, before it is published. A doubt is
  rejected, never published: the channel is looked for again later.
  :::

  :::field{name="Never proposed again" type="memory"}
  A logo refused in review is recorded with its reason (`data/rejected.csv`).
  :::
::

## Kept up to date

- Every week, a slice of the published logos is compared with the channel's website, and with the TV guides' logos:
  a new branding found there replaces the old logo.
- Small logos (under 1,000 px from a bitmap) are looked for again in a sharper version.
- A channel renamed at iptv-org is listed every Monday, and its logo is checked.
