## Where the logos come from

| Source | Used for |
|---|---|
| Contributors | logo requests through the [issue forms](https://github.com/worldwide-tv-logos/database/issues/new/choose) |
| [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org/database) | the logos listed with each channel |
| [iptv-epg](https://iptv-epg.org) | the logos of its TV guides, by country (with their permission) |
| [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org) / Wikimedia Commons | official logos with their dates (old ones are refused) |
| The channel's own website | its current logo |

## Preparation

Every candidate goes through the same steps:

- SVG originals are rendered at 1200 px; bitmaps are never upscaled.
- The image is trimmed to the drawing, its white background is removed (kept only when the logo would be
  unreadable on a dark screen without it) and the PNG is compressed without loss.
- The longest side must be at least 500 px once trimmed.
- A logo that is dark on a transparent background gets a **white variant**; a logo made of white letters gets a
  dark version, so that both kinds stay readable on any screen.

## Choice and review

- The best candidate is kept: SVG first, then the largest.
- Old brandings are refused: a Wikidata logo with an end date, or a file named after a past period.
- The same drawing found for several channels is a doubt (perceptual fingerprint), not a logo.
- Every logo is looked at on contact sheets, on a dark and a light background, before it is published. A logo
  refused in review is recorded and never proposed again.
