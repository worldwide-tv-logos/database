Getting started

TV guides

Find the channel and logo of a channel of your programme guide (EPG) from the ID the guide uses.

A TV guide (EPG, XMLTV) gives each channel an ID of its own, such as TF1+1.fr or France3-Alsace.fr. You don't need to build your own matching with our channels: it is done here every night, and published in guides.json.

Try it

Try:

The search above, and the one of the whole website (⌘Ctrl K), find a channel by its guide IDs from a score of 0.9. An edition's guide IDs lead to its channel's page, where the edition is listed.

How guide channels are matched

Every night, the channel list of each guide is read again and matched with our channels, in this order:

A confirmed alias

The guide ID was linked to a channel by review (aliases). Score 1.0; it always wins.

The same iptv-org ID

Many guides use the iptv-org ID of the channel (TF1.fr), case aside. Score 0.95.

The same name in the country

One channel only of the guide's country has this name. Quality and timeshift marks are left aside (TF1 HD, TF1 + 1 and TF1 are the same channel, with the same logo), and so is a final TV. A guide without channel names gives its ID's (France3.fr). Score 0.9.

Another name, or another country

The name is only one of the channel's other names, or the channel is from another country. Score 0.7: likely, not sure (other names are sometimes loose).

Ambiguous

Two or three channels have the name: each is listed with the score 0.5. A name shared by more than three channels is left out.

Use the matches of 0.9 and more directly. Show the others to a person to choose, or ask for an alias once you know the right channel: it becomes 1.0 for everyone.

A guide that renames its IDs is followed without anything to do: its new list is matched the next night. That is why guide IDs are not stored with the channels (except confirmed aliases).

Your guide is not listed

Match it by name: give the channel name and its country to the search, or look the name up in channels.json (name and aliases, in the same country). The rules above apply the same way.
