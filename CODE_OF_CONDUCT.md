# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, review requests on their merits, and keep discussions about the logos and
the data. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

Report a problem by opening an issue titled "Conduct" (without details in public), or by writing to a maintainer.
Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close issues, and block accounts that do not follow these rules.
